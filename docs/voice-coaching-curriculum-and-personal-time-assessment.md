# Dove Expressions Voice Coaching: Instructor Curriculum & Assessments
Status: DRAFT — instructor review required; not yet a published form, assigned lesson, or production migration.
Date: 2026-10-30

## Student-directed approach
- Start with a vocal consultation to discover student goals and recommend relevant learning areas.
- Follow up before evaluation with a core vocal intake. Mention available areas including Vocal Foundations, Song & Performance, Harmony, and Creative Worship Expression without presuming religious beliefs.
- Allow multiple selections, including no specialized religious coaching.
- Only students who opt into **Prophetic Worship Development** within Creative Worship Expression receive questions on prophetic reception, private spiritual practices, discernment or speaking/singing in tongues. The student can decline every religious activity without loss of core vocal coaching.
- Keep faith-related answers private to authorized instructors. Do not score, rank or label spiritual maturity.
- Instructor confirms lesson priorities using intake + observed vocal evaluation, not a rigid assignment schedule.
- The 12-week program may include structured multimedia and instructor-created practice tracks, when these are authored and ready. Do not promise unpublished content to students.

## Standard pathway structure
For every track: (1) student goals and baseline questionnaire; (2) coach observation; (3) model/explain; (4) guided drills; (5) application in song; (6) recorded homework and reflection; (7) reassess and document progress.

|Track|Initial assessment|Lesson teaching and guided drills|Song application/homework|Progress marker|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Posture & breath coordination|Alignment, tension, phrase length, breath behavior|Neutral posture; release tension; controlled S/V exhalation; coordinated inhalation; consonant/vowel phrases|Sing short and then longer lyrical phrases comfortably; log breath points|More consistent phrasing without forcing|
|Pitch accuracy & ear training|Match single piano notes, intervals, scales, key stability|Echo notes; 1-3-5-3-1; slow intervals; singing after playback; self-correction|Record an a cappella phrase and with accompaniment|Improved pitch matching and recovery|
|Vocal tone/openness|Breathiness, nasality, resonance, vowel clarity, comfort|Gentle hum, Vvv, vowel modification, consistent onset and resonance exploration|Compare two recordings of a familiar phrase|Clearer, more consistent tone without strain|
|Range & register control|Lowest/highest usable notes, comfortable tessitura, register shifts|Range map; descending/ascending comfortable patterns; chest/mix/head exploration without forcing|Practice register shifts in a chosen song|Expanded usable notes and smoother transitions|
|Agility & riffs|Note separation, tempo, accuracy, stylistic vocabulary|Echo simple runs; slow accurate patterns; rhythmic groupings; increase tempo gradually|Create two age/style appropriate riffs within a song|Clean notes at increasing controlled speeds|
|Harmony|Hold a part, hear intervals and group blend|Root/third/fifth reference; duet echo; split parts; recover after lead changes|Record harmony over a guide track|Independent part accuracy|
|Stamina & dynamics|Fatigue, phrase length, dynamic control|Efficient volume changes, sustainable repetition, rest pacing, controlled expression|Sing verse through chorus while varying dynamics|Reliable phrases and less fatigue|
|Song interpretation|Song familiarity, storytelling, phrasing, rhythm|Lyric meaning; phrasing choices; dynamic map; transitions; microphone practice|Before/after performance clip|Clear phrasing and expressive intentionality|
|Confidence & performance|Preparation, stage comfort, facial/body expression|Small mock performances, rehearsal routines, feedback and repeat|Submit video performance|More consistent delivery and recovery from mistakes|
|Ad-libbing & improvisation|Melody invention, lyric invention, in-key singing, returning to lead|Vary known lines, respond melodically to a lead, short lyrical improvisation, call-and-response|Add 2 purposeful lines over recorded worship song|Musical fluency without losing key or timing|
|Worship leading & flow (opt-in)|Transitions, exhortation, congregational guidance|Setlist linking, spoken Scripture, clear cues, musical handoffs, responsive leadership|Prepare and record a transition/exhortation|Clarity and musical continuity|
|Prophetic worship development (opt-in)|Private practice, spiritual reception self-report, discernment, spontaneous singing skills|Scripture to declaration; preparation vs spontaneity; instrumental free flow; thoughtful review|Practice Scripture-infused singing in private, with instrumental only or over a known song|Student-defined confidence and faithful handling of Scripture|

## Specialized Creative Worship Expression
Three selectable interests:
A. **Worship Leadership & Flow** — transitions, exhortation, leadership, Scripture integration.
B. **Spontaneous Vocal Expression** — ad-libs, new melodies/lyrics, call & response.
C. **Prophetic Worship Development** — student-requested faith-based exploration including hearing God, discernment, private worship foundation, scripture-based declarations, free-flow singing; optional tongues-related teaching only by explicit request.

### Prophetic worship workflow
1. Start with the student's goals; do not presume their beliefs or spiritual history.
2. Send *Personal Time With God* questionnaire (below) and the existing prophetic reception test after the instructor reviews that test. The two forms are separate.
3. Discuss the student's private worship and Scripture foundation before coaching congregational prophetic flow. Avoid treating time frequency as a grade.
4. Observe ad-libbing and call-and-response separately from instrumental-only free flow.
5. Teach Scripture-to-song: pray, select a passage relevant to the song, read in context, identify a true encouraging statement, shape a declaration or sung phrase, and check faithful interpretation.
   Example: Luke 22:31-32, Jesus tells Simon that Satan desired to sift him and that Jesus prayed for his faith. Encourage with the actual passage rather than implying the singer speaks as Jesus.
6. Practice modes:
   - **Ad-lib over a song:** introduce new words/melodies while the song continues.
   - **Call and response:** create responses in intentional gaps.
   - **Instrumental-only free flow:** no lead or background vocals; create singing/words from scratch.
   - **Scripture to song:** prepare declarations before a song and, optionally, improvise.
   - **Singing in tongues:** only for students who identify with and request this practice. The coaching reflects their denominational convictions, never presents a contested practice as a requirement, and honors consent.
7. Potential 12-week multimedia: licensed/created background tracks, optional Suno compositions, guided and open-ended practice clips. Must be created and reviewed before enrollment promises.
8. Revisit student's own goals and comfort; no judgment or spiritual rating.

Scripture references for instructor's content: John 15:4-5 (abiding); 1 Corinthians 14:3 (edification, encouragement and comfort); Colossians 3:16 (singing God's word); 1 Corinthians 14:15 (sing with spirit and understanding); 1 Thessalonians 5:20-21 (weigh and test).

## Personal Time With God — STUDENT QUESTIONNAIRE DRAFT
**Audience:** only students who explicitly choose Prophetic Worship Development.
**Purpose:** Help your coach understand your current practices and tailor support. There are no right answers and no judgment. You may skip any question. Responses should be visible only to you and your coach, not other students.

1. How often do you currently make intentional time for prayer, worship, Scripture, or quiet reflection? [Daily / 4–6 days weekly / 2–3 days weekly / About weekly / Occasionally / Rarely / Prefer not to answer]
2. How much time do you usually spend in a session? [Under 10 minutes / 10–20 / 21–40 / More than 40 / Varies / Prefer not to answer]
3. What activities are part of that time? [Check all: Prayer / Scripture reading / Scripture study / Singing or worship music / Quiet listening / Journaling / Other / Prefer not to answer]
4. How often do you sing spontaneously or improvise during private worship? [Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never / Unsure / Prefer not to answer]
5. Do you currently use instrumental worship music in your personal time? [Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never / Prefer not to answer]
6. How comfortable are you with expressing a Scripture through your own sung words? [Very / Somewhat / Not yet / Unsure / Prefer not to answer]
7. Have you experienced what you personally understand as prophetic impressions, words, images, or utterances? [Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never / Unsure / Prefer not to answer]
8. When you believe you receive something from God, how do you usually reflect on or discern it? [Optional short answer]
9. What helps you connect with God during personal worship? [Optional short answer]
10. What, if anything, feels challenging about personal worship or spontaneous singing? [Optional short answer]
11. What would you like to grow in through coaching? [Check all: consistency in private worship / Scripture-to-song / spontaneous melody / lyrical ad-libs / call & response / discernment / leading a congregation / other]
12. Are there any spiritual practices or topics you do NOT want included in your coaching? [Optional short answer]
13. Is there anything else you want your instructor to know about your worship background or beliefs? [Optional short answer]

**Separate, conditional follow-up** for students who specifically request singing in tongues (not displayed on general intake): 'Would you like coaching on singing in tongues as part of your worship practice?' [Yes / No / Unsure]. 'Is there anything about your church tradition or beliefs you'd like your coach to respect?' [Optional short answer]. Never treat participation as a competence requirement.

## Stephanie case study (instructor-only)
Known interest: prophetic flow, ad-libbing, worship expression. Do not infer frequency of private worship, level of discernment, practice of tongues or technical competency without her responses/evaluation.
- Send selected spiritual questionnaire and review the separately supplied reception test.
- Baseline: observe lyrical ad-libs over a familiar song, call & response, and free-flow over an instrumental as separate exercises.
- Select which needs foundational teaching and which can advance.
- Begin with Scripture-to-song as one accessible practice, not a prerequisite that all students must follow.
- Assign practice clips and review progress with student permission.
